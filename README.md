# Online Data Collection

Lecture notes from the **Azmi Noteworthy Series** for the Advanced Diploma in Applied Epidemiology for Disease Control and Outbreak Management.

Live site: https://sahrolazmi.github.io/online-data-collection-aded/

Topics: what online data collection is, paper vs digital evidence, challenges, planning a digital form, ethics and Malaysian law, choosing a tool, good and bad practice, case studies (including WHO EWARS in the Rohingya camps), a quick quiz and 28 references.
